Privacy policy

Effective 4 October 2026

This policy describes the data processed by the Tiramisu Review app (Android and iOS) and this website. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for people in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Controller

Agama SA, chemin des Vieilles Cibles 5, 3960 Sierre, Switzerland (UID CHE-423.695.975).
Contact: info@agama.ch

In short

  • No account to create, no e-mail or password asked.
  • No ads, no ad tracking, no data sale.
  • Your reviews are public, under a nickname.
  • Your location is used to find places around you; it is not stored with your identity.
  • You can delete everything from the app, at any time.

Legal bases

Processing reviews, places and nicknames is necessary to provide the service you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Crash reports, technical logs and moderation rely on our legitimate interest in running a safe and reliable service (Art. 6(1)(f)). Location is only used with your permission, which you can withdraw in your device settings.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or port your data, and object to processing, by writing to info@agama.ch. Full deletion is also available in the app (how to). You can lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, in the European Union, with your country’s authority.

Children

Tiramisu Review is not intended for children under 13.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Data access is restricted by per-user rules: everyone can only change their own reviews.

Changes

Any change is published on this page with its effective date. Important changes are announced in the app.
