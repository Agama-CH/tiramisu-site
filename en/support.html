Help and contact

Write to info@agama.ch. We usually answer within 48 hours, and within 24 hours for objectionable content.

How do I rate a tiramisu?

Open the Add tab, pick the restaurant from the nearby list or search it by name, then give your score out of 10. Photo and details are optional.

I’m rating a tiramisu I ate elsewhere

In the Add tab, tap “I’m not there right now” and type the city: the list and the search then start from that city. Pick the tasting date under the score.

The restaurant is not in the list

Tap “Not in the list? Add a place”, type its name and put the pin on the map.

A place is closed or has moved

Open the place page, ⋯ menu, then “Report this place”.

A review is objectionable

⋯ menu of the review, then “Report”. You can also block its author: their reviews disappear for you. We review every report within 24 hours and remove content that breaks the terms of use.

Delete my data

Profile tab, “Delete my account and data”. Details on the Account deletion page.
